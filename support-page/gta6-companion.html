Contact

Questions, a code that does not work, or a place missing from the map? Write to apustovidko.dev@gmail.com. We usually reply within two days.

Common questions

  • My cheat code does nothing. Every cheat is locked until you finish the story, and most also need their own challenge. Open the cheat in the app to see what is left.
  • Where is a code that says "not found yet"? Players have not confirmed it yet. It appears in the app as soon as it is, with no update needed.
  • Restore a purchase. Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple ID.
  • Cancel a subscription. Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions.
