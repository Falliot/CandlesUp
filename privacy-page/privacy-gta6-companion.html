Cheats & Map for GTA 6 - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Your Data Stays on Your Device

Your progress, saved cheats, visited places, language and console choice are stored only on your device. The app has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not upload, store or process this information on our servers.

No Analytics, No Ads, No Tracking

The app does not include analytics, advertising networks or tracking tools, and it does not collect names, email addresses or contact information.

In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. We use RevenueCat to check and manage purchases and subscriptions. RevenueCat receives transaction information and an anonymous app user identifier, and may receive device information needed to do this. This data is processed according to RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Content Updates

The app downloads a small file with newly found cheat codes and notes from a page hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any website, the host may log technical details such as your IP address when the file is requested. This is covered by GitHub's privacy statement.

Third-Party Services

We use RevenueCat for purchase management and GitHub Pages to host content updates. We do not sell your data or share it for marketing.

Your Privacy Control

If you delete the app, all data it stored on your device is removed.

Unofficial App

This is an unofficial, fan-made guide. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

Questions?
