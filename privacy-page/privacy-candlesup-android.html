CandlesUp for Android - Privacy Policy

CandlesUp keeps your birthday list on your phone. There is no account to create, and we run no server that stores your birthdays, names, photos or contacts.

Your birthdays stay on your device

Names, birthdays, photos, email addresses, phone numbers, notes, reminders and settings are stored only on your phone. We cannot see them and we never receive them.

Backup

If Android Auto Backup is turned on for your phone, Android may copy the app's data to your Google account and restore it when you set up a new phone. Google makes and keeps that copy under your Google account settings, not us. You can turn it off in your phone's system settings.

Usage analytics

We use PostHog to learn which features are used and to improve the app. It receives a random ID created when you first open the app, events such as "birthday added" with simple details (counts, yes/no values, a screen or feature name), and your answers to the questions during setup. It never receives names, birth dates, photos, notes, email addresses, phone numbers, or the contents of your contacts or calendar. As with any internet service, it also sees your IP address, the app version, your phone model and your Android version. PostHog also uses the IP address to work out your approximate location (country and city).

Crash reports

If the app crashes, Sentry receives a report with the error details, the app version, your phone model, your Android version and the same random ID. It also records the timing of a small share of app start-ups and screens. Reports are not meant to contain your birthday data.

Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases go through Google Play. We use RevenueCat to check whether Premium is active and to restore it. RevenueCat receives your purchase information from Google Play, a random user ID and the random analytics ID above, so purchases can be matched with usage. We never see your payment card or Google account details.

Contacts and calendar

Only when you open "Import from Contacts" or "Import from Calendar" and allow access, CandlesUp reads birthdays and names on your phone (for contacts also the photo, phone number and email address) and saves the ones you choose in the app. This data is not sent to us or to anyone else. You can withdraw the permission at any time in your phone's settings.

Microphone

The Digital Candle listens through the microphone while its screen is open, only to notice when you blow. The sound is turned into a volume level on the spot and discarded: it is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. The permission is asked for only when you open the candle.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your phone with Android's alarm system. On Android 13 and later they need the notification permission. The app also asks to be told when the phone restarts, so it can schedule your reminders again.

Other internet use

Besides the services above, the app loads the feature list from GitHub Pages and sends your votes to the counter service tally.fly.dev in Feature Voting. A vote contains only the feature number, with no ID. Those hosts can see your IP address. Photo credits and gift ideas open in your browser, and those sites follow their own policies. The gift search is a generic "birthday gift" search and never contains a person's name.

Famous birthdays

The names and birth dates of famous people are part of the app and work offline. Photos come from Wikimedia Commons under their own licences.

No ads, no selling

CandlesUp shows no ads, does not use the Android advertising ID, and does not sell or share your data for advertising.

Children

CandlesUp is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your choices

You can delete any birthday inside the app. Uninstalling the app, or choosing "Clear storage" in Android's app settings, removes everything stored on your phone. You can turn off each permission at any time. To ask us to delete analytics, crash or purchase records connected to your device, email us.

Questions

If you have questions about this policy, write to apustovidko.dev@gmail.com. If we change the policy, the new text appears here with a new date.
